# OnlyBudget — notes for future sessions

Read README.md first. The five product principles there are non-negotiable and every change must respect them: no bank linking, voice and text equal, no dark patterns, local-first with backup, calm not gamified.

- Pure logic (parser, plain-language sentences, recurring schedule, export, crypto) lives in `src/parser` and `src/lib` and must stay free of React Native imports so `npm test` covers it.
- The parser never saves anything on its own. Every path ends at `ConfirmationCard`.
- Copy in the UI is plain sentences. No "over budget!", no red states, no streaks.
- Every user-facing string comes from `useT()` / the locale tables in `src/i18n/locales`. Adding a key means adding it to all eight locale files (the `LocaleDef` type enforces it). Parser behavior per language lives in `src/parser/packs`.
- Schema changes go in a new entry of `MIGRATIONS` in `src/db/database.ts`. Never edit an existing migration.
- Budget periods: the Today and Budget screens show one `Period` (`{start, end}`), the calendar month by default or payday-to-payday when `settings.budgetPeriod` is `pay`. Paydays come only from active repeating income rules, and only from the main pay (`mainPayRules`: the largest and any at least half its size, so a side gig never splits a period) (`src/lib/periods.ts`); with none, pay mode falls back to the month. Category limits, the optional overall plan (`settings.overallLimit`) and roll-over all apply per period. Roll-over (`category.rollover`: reset, carry, ask) is computed in `src/lib/rollover.ts` from earlier periods on or after `rolloverSince`; only unspent money carries, never a shortfall; changing the plan of a `carry` category saves what had carried as the decision for the current period and moves `rolloverSince` there (`saveCategory`), and `carryInto` starts from that seed; "ask" answers live in the `carryovers` table keyed by period start. Removing an entry never shows a dialog: the `UndoBar` on the tabs puts it back (`lastDeleted` in the store). "delete that" by voice shows no answer card either: the undo bar is the receipt, read aloud only under the spoken-answer rule; a second one answers `correctionNothingToRemove`. Today also carries `BackupNotice` (`src/lib/backupNudge.ts`: after `NUDGE_AFTER_ENTRIES` with no backup, or a backup older than `STALE_BACKUP_DAYS`; "Not now" snoozes `SNOOZE_DAYS`, the day kept in `app_state`).
- Parser kinds: transaction, goal (kind saving or debt), contribution, reminder, question. Reminder phrasing per language lives in the `reminder` block of each pack (lead words, clock times, repeat words, future days); shared helpers are in `src/parser/reminderRules.ts`. Notifications are scheduled only from `src/lib/reminders.ts`, and anything that saves a reminder must handle the permission being refused (tell the user, keep the reminder).
- Questions ("how much have I spent this week on Starbucks?") are parsed by `src/parser/questionRules.ts` using the `question` block of each pack (lead words, income lead words, period words, leftovers to strip). A question is never saved: `src/lib/questions.ts` works out the range (weeks start on the locale's `format.firstDay`: Sunday in en/ja/ko, Monday in es/fr/de/it/zh, threaded through `periodRange`, `compareRanges`, `weekRange`, `isRecapDay` and the calendar grid) and total, the sentence comes from `answerTotal`/`answerNone` in the locale, and `src/lib/speak.ts` reads it aloud with the phone's own voices when the `answerMode` setting says so (`auto` = aloud only when asked by voice). Matching a place or thing is a plain substring test; never build a RegExp from the target. "What's left" questions ("what's my remaining budget?", "how much do I have left for groceries?") are `ask: 'left'`, matched by each pack's `remainingLead` before the spending lead (some spending leads, like zh `多少`, would also match), and answered by the pure `answerRemaining` with the same numbers as `PeriodCard`: overall plan, else income less bills still due (`safeToSpend`), else the category plans added up; a category answers from its own plan with carry-over. The period `budget` is the current budget period (payday to payday in pay mode, else the month), said as "this pay period" or "since payday"; a spending question with no period word uses it too (`periodDefaulted`). `weekend` is the most recent Saturday and Sunday (this one while it is under way) and `recentDays` the last `days` days, read by each pack's `recentDays` regex before the period words. With a digit left in the sentence a "what's left" lead does not count, so an amount always makes an entry. Each pack's `groups` maps an everyday word to several categories ("food" = groceries + dining), matched only as the whole target and after the user's own category names; then a word that starts a category name ("dining" = "Dining out") matches that category.
- Fixing by voice is the parser kind `correction` (each pack's `correction.remove`, matched against the whole utterance, and `correction.lead`, which must start it and may be followed only by a new amount, category or date; a spending or income verb after the lead makes it a new entry). A correction may name its entry (`correction.target`, from the pack's `removeTarget` / `targetLeads`: "forget the spotify one", "that uber was 32 not 23", "change the uber to 32"); `useEntryFlow` then finds the newest entry whose note or category contains those words (plain substring, never a RegExp) and answers `correctionNoMatch` when none does. Without a target, `useEntryFlow.applyCorrection` changes an open card, or opens the entry saved last (`lastSavedId` in the store, else the newest by `created_at`) in the same `ConfirmationCard` with `editOf` set, so the person still saves it; "delete that" removes it at once and the `UndoBar` puts it back. Once the entry saved last is removed (by voice or by hand), `lastSavedId` is `''`, so a second "delete that" never reaches for an older entry; an undo makes the restored entry the target again. A transfer logged by `contributeToGoal` carries `goalId` (v16), and editing, removing or restoring it moves the goal by the same amount. Three more question kinds share the question block: `compare` (`compareLead`; this week, month, year or pay period so far against the same point in the one before, `compareRanges`), `top` (`topLead`; the biggest category, `answerTop`) and `afford` (`affordLead`; the amount is taken out before the lead is matched again and becomes `price`, weighed by `answerAfford` against the category plan when there is one, else what is left overall; without an amount it is a `left` question). `askWords` drops leftovers from their targets. Answers state numbers, never a yes or no.
- Onboarding's money page (optional) turns take-home pay, how often and the last payday into a repeating income rule starting on that payday, switches the budget period to paydays, and turns rent and its day into a monthly bill this month, so Today has a real number at once. For the first `REVIEW_DAYS` of a new period Today shows `periodReview` (`src/lib/review.ts`, `review` in the store): what went out against the period before, what came in and was kept, the three biggest categories and how each moved. It and the weekly recap appear only when the first entry was saved before the span they look back on, so a new phone never gets a look back at days nobody used it.
- Phrasings: `__tests__/phrasings/<lang>.test.ts` hold many everyday ways of saying the same thing (spend, income, questions, what is left, goals, contributions, reminders) with what each must become, run through the shared `harness.ts` with the language's category names. When a person says something the app misreads, add the sentence there first, then fix the pack. Each file also has entries that must stay entries, so a looser question lead never swallows "lunch 12 total". Packs may set `question.weakLead` (a spending question only when the sentence has no digit: "coffee total this month", "spending on gas") and `goalNouns` (generic words around a goal name: "the Japan fund"). Optional pack fields: `countStop`, the words that may follow a bare number without making it a count, so "2 coffees 8" takes 8 as the money and keeps "2 coffees" in the note (`extractAmount`, only with nothing marked and a later number; the CJK packs do not need it, counters are skipped in `findAmounts`); `perPeriod` ("200 a month", "每月2万"), which makes a goal's amount a pace: the amount is dropped, `hint.goalPace` asks for the total, and a bare "save 50 a week" still reads as a goal; `correction.notTail` ("not 12", "non 12"), the number being corrected, dropped before the plain lead is read (the named forms see the whole sentence); `correction.removeTarget` and `targetLeads` for naming the entry, now in every language. A custom category's whole name is matched before any dictionary phrase, however long ("... and school supplies" is one category, not Shopping). A category matched through `singularForms` reports the words as said, and a spending verb is removed from the note as a whole word only. Every language's phrasing file now holds 500 to 700 everyday sentences, including putting money away, slang, regional words, dictation quirks, questions as people ask them, named corrections and spoken reminder times; when adding more, run that language's file first, then `npx jest __tests__/phrasings`.
- The web build must keep working: `npm run build:web` (Metro config adds the SQLite wasm asset; nginx/dev server send COOP/COEP headers). Use `confirmDialog`/`notify` from `src/lib/dialogs.ts` instead of `Alert`, which is a no-op on web.
- Statement import: parsing is pure and lives in `src/statements` (rows → dates/money → lines → months; credit reports → tradelines; pay-off planner). PDF text extraction is the only platform-specific part (`src/components/PdfExtractor.tsx` runs pdf.js in a no-network WebView built by `scripts/build-pdf-extractor.js`; `.web.tsx` uses pdfjs-dist with the worker copied to `public/`). Never send a statement anywhere. Merchant vocabulary for statements is `STATEMENT_MERCHANTS` in `src/statements/merchants.ts`. Numeric dates ("03/04") follow the statement itself (`numericDateOrder`: "15/03" anywhere means day first), else the phone's region (`defaultDateOrder`, `deviceRegion()`); yen and won amounts need no cents; on a card statement with no signs or sections, plain payment and refund lines are credits. An import is one transaction and can be taken back in one go (`removeImport`, listed on the import screen).
- Security rules (see docs/SECURITY.md): anything that crosses a trust boundary (user text, imported files, model replies) goes through `src/lib/validate.ts`, in both directions — text sent to Smart Assist is `redactSensitive`d first, so a card or account number never leaves the phone; SQL is always parameterised (a statement assembled into a variable may interpolate only a clause this file built from literals, never a user value) and `%`/`_` typed into a search box are escaped, not wildcards; never build a RegExp from user data; no new outbound hosts; the web page has no inline scripts (CSP). `__tests__/security.test.ts` enforces these, and `npm run check:web-security` loads the web build under the real headers in headless Chromium.
- Voices (`settings.voice`: plain, friend, coach, roaster; `settings.attitude`: nice, playful, spicy) add one line after a save, chosen by the pure engine in `src/lib/personas` from English templates in `lines.en.ts` (other app languages get nothing until their lines exist): eight generic lines per voice, situation and attitude, plus flavour lines for coffee, restaurants, delivery, shopping, subscriptions and entertainment (`flavorFor` reads the note and category; a flavour line gets what is left appended when the category has a plan). The last 24 line keys are kept in the `app_state` table so nothing repeats soon, even across launches. Hard rules live in the engine and its tests: plain says nothing; necessities (`PROTECTED_CATEGORIES` plus a name check for custom categories) never get a line; nothing is said past a category plan or the overall plan; amounts at or above `BIG_AMOUNT` are always nice; only `TEASABLE_CATEGORIES` may be spicy. Lines target the latte, never the person. The card passes the line to the Today toast, which reads it aloud under the same rule as answers to questions. Onboarding asks once whether the user wants a voice (`VoicePicker` is shared with Settings → Voice); tapping the line on the toast opens that screen. Spoken switching ("switch to the coach", "female voice", "plain voice") is the parser kind `command` from `src/parser/commandRules.ts` (English only): applied at once in `useEntryFlow`, never saved, answered with the voice's greeting. The spoken voice's gender (`settings.voiceGender`) is picked from the phone's own voices by name in `src/lib/voices.ts`; `speak()` takes it as its third argument.
- Screens show numbers and key words, not sentences; the sentences in `locale.sentences` are for the voice (spoken answers, greetings) and for tests. History pages its list (`PAGE` in `app/(tabs)/history.tsx`, "Show more" for the next page) while the line above it counts and totals every match in SQL, so it is true whatever the list has loaded. In pay mode its first two chips are this and the last pay period (same `periodContaining`/`shiftPeriod` as Today), so its totals match the period card. `PeriodCard` (Today and Budget) is one big number that answers "what is left?" (plan remaining, else income minus spending and bills still due until the period ends, else spending), one line of in and out, the spending bar and a short label; category rows use `planShort`/`pastPlanShort`/`carriedShort`, goals use `goalShort` plus a pace word. Today also shows one spending bar (`SpendBar`: the biggest categories as segments of one hue, darkest first, 2px gaps, names underneath; colour follows size, identity comes from the labels), a "safe to spend until payday" line (`src/lib/safeToSpend.ts`: income minus spending minus repeating bills still due in the period, minus `reserveForLater`: what a bill in the next 31 days needs from now because the paychecks before it cannot cover it, only with repeating income; only with income in the period), and on Sunday and Monday a recap of last week (`src/lib/recap.ts`, weeks start Sunday; `recap` in the store). Headline sentences use Fraunces (loaded in the root layout, system face if it fails); totals count up and progress bars settle via `src/components/motion.tsx`, which honours reduced motion. `settings.accent` picks the hue family (`ACCENTS` in the theme); everything derives from it.
- Each expense category has a `tier` (`needs`, `obligations`, `wants`; `obligations` is shown as "Debts", since the stored id never changes but the label is a locale string), kept in `src/lib/tiers.ts` with `tierTotals` and migrations v13/v14. These are the three buckets of the 50/30/20 rule, so no label needs explaining; a fourth "comforts" tier was tried and cut because no short word carried the distinction. Defaults are derived so nobody configures anything: the known categories are placed by id, a custom category whose name matches `NECESSITY_WORDS` starts on `needs`, anything else a person created starts on `wants`. `other_expense` and spending with no category count as `needs`: unidentified money is protected rather than called negotiable. Savings is a `need`, so paying yourself is never shown as the leftover. The Budget screen shows the three totals with a bar each; it states the split and never advises cutting anything; the only place a question is asked is `tierHint`, read while sorting a category, never beside a total. `tierOf` falls back to the default whenever the stored value is missing or unknown, so an old backup restores fine. Pay-yourself-first needs no extra code: a repeating savings transfer is an expense rule, so `safeToSpend` already subtracts it before offering anything as spendable.
- The free month and the purchase: `src/lib/trial.ts` is pure (`licenseFrom`: first and latest day seen in `app_state`, clock set back never adds days, `TRIAL_DAYS`, `NOTICE_DAYS`); `src/lib/purchases.ts` is the only module that imports `expo-iap` (products `full_unlock` and the iOS-only free `trial_30`; `purchases.web.ts` answers unlocked, the web build is never gated); the store keeps `license` and reads it in `refreshLicense` at start, on resume and after Erase (which keeps the three `app_state` keys). Once `ended`, only adding stops: `useAdding()` from `src/components/TrialGate.tsx` guards the Today bar, new goals, new repeating entries, imports and new debts with the `TrialEnded` card; `TrialNotice` is one dismissable line in the last `NOTICE_DAYS`. Reading, questions, edits, backups and exports are never gated. The purchase screen is `settings/unlock` (Settings → About → Your purchase), with Buy at the store's `displayPrice` and Restore.
- Release switches live in `src/lib/features.ts` (pure constants). `SMART_ASSIST` is false for 1.0: the Settings row and About section are hidden, `useEntryFlow` never calls `llmParse`, and the `settings/smart` screen (deep link only) shows `smartNotYet`; the code and its security tests stay. Flipping it means restoring the Smart parsing sentence in every locale's "No ads" commitment and redoing `store/listing.md` and `docs/PRIVACY.md`. The name is changed everywhere with `npm run rename -- "Name" com.example.id` (`scripts/rename-app.mjs`, `--dry-run` first).
- Shipping: `eas.json` has the build profiles (`preview` is an Android APK that needs no store account — the quickest way onto a real phone) and `docs/RELEASE.md` is the whole route from here to a listing, including what only a device can test. The privacy policy is `docs/PRIVACY.md` and the support page `docs/SUPPORT.md`, rendered to self-contained HTML by `npm run build:privacy`; edit the Markdown, never the HTML. They are hosted from the separate public repository `mradsoftware/mradsoftware.github.io` (`npm run build:site` packages them with its Pages workflow into `out/site`), so the policy URL never depends on this repository. `src/lib/links.ts` holds `PRIVACY_URL`, empty until the policy is hosted, which hides the button on the About screen rather than shipping a dead link; it is the one file allowed to name an address, and only ever to hand to `Linking.openURL`. Settings → Erase everything calls `resetAll`, which cancels scheduled notifications, wipes every table, puts the settings row back to its defaults (onboarding included, so currency and language are asked again) and clears the Smart Assist key from secure storage.
- The app ships empty. Sample data lives only in `scripts/lib/shots.mjs` (shared by the `ui:scenario`, `ui:persona` and `ui:sweep` screenshot scripts) and reaches a browser session through the backup restore screen; `__tests__/release.test.ts` checks that app code never imports the scripts, carries no scenario markers, and that migrations insert only categories and settings. In-app instructions are the Help screen (`app/settings/help.tsx`, strings `helpIntro`/`helpTopics` in every locale); update a topic when its feature changes.
- Goals keep `startingAmount` (v15, set on insert by `upsertGoal`, never changed after): pace counts only money added since, and is judged only after `PACE_AFTER_DAYS`; before that, or with nothing added, the status is `no_pace` and screens show no pace word.
- Reminders (`src/lib/reminders.ts`) use a repeating trigger where the platform has one (daily, weekly, monthly on days 1–28) and otherwise a dated series of `SERIES_LENGTH` notifications (`id~k`), topped up by `rearmReminders`, which runs at start, on resume and after a restore. Coming back to the app calls `resume()`: one catch-up of repeating rules (single-flight), the screens move to today's period, reminders re-armed.
- Dictation writes "four fifty" as "4 50" and "five and a half" as words: `findAmounts` (`mergeDictated`) joins a whole number and exactly two digits with one space between into one amount, and adds a half, in the space-tokenized languages; a time word just before ("at 9 30") stops the merge, and the clock rule reads "9 30" as 9:30. A goal whose name would be empty because the occasion set its date ("save 500 for christmas") is named after the occasion. A spoken currency that is not the app's (`spokenCurrency`) adds `hint.currencyDiffers`; `$`/dollars are too ambiguous to count. Spanish `bareIncomeIsPay`: an income verb with nothing else said ("me pagaron 2400") is salary.
- Money typed into a field goes through `parseMoneyInput` (last separator plus one or two digits is the decimal; three digits group thousands; null past `MAX_AMOUNT`); a field with text that does not read shows `amountUnreadable` through `Field`'s `error` and nothing is saved. Dates are picked from `DatePicker`'s month calendar (first day per locale); clock times show through `clockLabel` (`format.hour12`: "3:00 PM" in English, "15:00" elsewhere) while stored as HH:MM; `future` and `mode` ('reminder', 'start') keep it on today or later.
- Voice input (`src/lib/speech.ts`) asks for on-device recognition when offered and falls back once to the ordinary recognizer when the on-device model is missing; on iOS it hands the audio session back when listening ends. `pickVoice` never chooses an Android `-network` voice, so answers are synthesised on the phone. Backup encryption uses `scryptAsync` so the screen keeps drawing.
- Platform protections (tested in `__tests__/security.test.ts`): iOS files use `NSFileProtectionComplete`; `plugins/withNoDataTransfer.js` keeps every file out of Android backup and device transfer (expo-secure-store's own rules are off so they do not compete); permissions a library adds and the app does not use go in `android.blockedPermissions`. With the app lock on, `LockGate` marks screens private (`expo-screen-capture`) and reminders carry a generic body (`reminderPrivateBody`); a clock set back relocks, and only `canUseAppLock()` being false lets the app open without asking (never saved as off). The PDF WebView has `originWhitelist={['*']}` so every navigation reaches the handler, which allows only the blank page; it is restarted after each read. Backups: at most `MAX_RULES` repeating entries, none before 2000, active ones more than a year behind restart within the year; ids that read as a route or an `Object.prototype` name are refused; catch-up logs at most `CATCH_UP_LIMIT` entries per run. Text that must keep a value uses `redactOrMask`, never `redactSensitive(x) || x`. SQLite runs with `secure_delete`; Erase ends with a WAL checkpoint and `VACUUM`. nginx repeats the full header set in every location, and fixed-name files (service worker, pdf.js worker and fonts) are `no-cache` and network-first in `public/sw.js`.
- Run `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` before committing. `npx expo export --platform android` is a good smoke test for bundling.
- UI rules from the UX review: `Text` with variant display/title/heading is an accessibility header and uses the system face in ja/ko/zh (Fraunces has no CJK glyphs); `Segmented` marks the chosen segment with `accentSoft` and an accent border (card-on-cardAlt was invisible in dark mode); every tappable is at least 44 points; rows of buttons wrap. The Budget tab folds categories with no spending and no plan behind "N more categories" once something has been spent; the spend bar's folded remainder is `spendRest` with a "⋯" glyph, never the Other category's icon. Settings order: Your data (backup with its date, budget period, repeating, reminders), Preferences, More (import, debts, learned words, offline voice, Smart parsing), About, Erase. `PeriodCard` names the bills kept back (`afterBillsShort`); the weekly recap compares only against a week the app was used in.
