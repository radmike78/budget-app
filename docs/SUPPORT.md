# OnlyBudget — Support

**MRAD Software Solutions LLC**

OnlyBudget is a budget you talk to. Say or type what you spent, confirm it, and
the app does the rest on your phone. There is no account, no bank linking, no
server and no ads.

## Contact

Questions, problems, or something the app misread:

**[support@mradsoftware.com](mailto:support@mradsoftware.com)**

When you write about a misread entry, include the sentence you said or typed
and what you expected; that is usually enough to fix it in the next release.

## Common questions

- **Where is my data?** On your phone, and nowhere else. See the
  [privacy policy](privacy.html).
- **How do I back up?** Settings → Backup & export. The backup is a file you keep in
  your own cloud drive, with an optional passphrase. Restore it from the same
  screen on a new phone.
- **Can I delete everything?** Settings → Erase everything removes it all at
  once. Uninstalling the app does the same. There is nothing to delete
  anywhere else, because nothing ever left the phone.
- **Is there a refund?** Purchases are made through the App Store or Google
  Play, and refunds go through them: Apple at
  [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), Google from the
  order in the Play Store.
- **Can I bring in my bank's history?** Yes, without linking: download a PDF statement or a CSV export from your bank's website and open it under Settings → Import a statement. Every line is shown for review before anything is saved, lines already in your history are flagged, and an import can be taken back in one go.
- **Does it link to my bank?** No, and it never will.
