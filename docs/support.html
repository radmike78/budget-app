OnlyBudget is a budget you talk to. Say or type what you spent, confirm it, and the app does the rest on your phone. There is no account, no bank linking, no server and no ads.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your phone, and nowhere else. See the privacy policy.
  • How do I back up? Settings → Back up. The backup is a file you keep in your own cloud drive, with an optional passphrase. Restore it from the same screen on a new phone.
  • Can I delete everything? Settings → Erase everything removes it all at once. Uninstalling the app does the same. There is nothing to delete anywhere else, because nothing ever left the phone.
  • Is there a refund? Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, and refunds go through them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google from the order in the Play Store.
  • Does it link to my bank? No, and it never will.
