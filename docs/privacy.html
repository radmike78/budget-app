Privacy Policy — OnlyBudget

Last updated: 19 October 2026

OnlyBudget is a budgeting app that runs entirely on your phone. It has no server and no account. We do not receive your budget, and there is no "we" that could: nothing you enter is transmitted to the developer of this app, at any point, for any reason.

This policy explains that in full, including the one case where something can leave your device and exactly what it involves.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up, and there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No bank linking. OnlyBudget never asks for bank credentials and has no ability to connect to a financial institution. Every amount is one you typed or spoke.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking of any kind.
  • No server. The developer operates no backend for this app. There is nowhere for your data to be sent, and no database of users.
  • Your data stays on your phone, in the app's own storage, until you delete it or uninstall the app.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is kept in a database inside the app's private storage on your device: entries and their amounts, dates, notes and categories; categories and their limits; goals; repeating entries; reminders; debts; records of statements you imported; and your settings.

On iOS and Android this storage is private to the app and is covered by the device's own encryption. On iOS the files are locked whenever the device is locked with a passcode. On the web version it is stored in your browser, under the origin the app is served from.

OnlyBudget never makes a copy of this data anywhere else; you make one yourself by exporting it (see Backups and exports). On Android the app is left out of Google's automatic backup and of transfers to a new phone. On iOS, a backup of the whole iPhone that you make to iCloud or to a computer includes the data of the apps on it, OnlyBudget's too, as for any app; that backup is yours and Apple's terms cover it, and OnlyBudget cannot read or reach it.

What leaves your device, and when

One feature involves a network, and only one. It is described here in full.

1. Speech recognition, when you use the microphone

When you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech recognizer turns what you say into text. OnlyBudget never records audio, never stores audio, and never sends audio anywhere itself.

The recognizer is part of your operating system, not part of this app:

  • Where the device supports on-device recognition, OnlyBudget asks for it, and the audio does not leave the phone.
  • Where it does not, the platform's recognizer may send the audio to Apple or Google for processing. That is a function of your operating system, and their privacy policy governs it, not this one. Your device settings control it.

Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested the first time you use the mic, and the microphone is active only while you are speaking an entry. You can use every feature of the app by typing instead.

2. Nothing else

This version of the app has no feature that sends anything off your phone. It makes no network requests of its own: there is no server, no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only outbound connection the app is built to be capable of, an optional parsing assist that would send a single unclear phrase to a language model with your own API key, is switched off in the code for this release and cannot be turned on from the app. If a later version offers it, this policy will describe it before it ships, it will be off by default, and turning it on will ask you first.

Statements you import

If you import a bank or card statement, or a credit report, as a PDF:

  • The file is read on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Text extraction runs inside a sandboxed view with no network access at all.
  • The file's bytes are wiped from memory as soon as the text has been read, and the app's copy of the file is deleted.
  • The file's name is not kept, because file names often contain account numbers.
  • Card, account, routing and IBAN numbers are removed from the text before any entry is saved. A line that reads PAYMENT TO 4123456789019876 VISA is stored as PAYMENT TO VISA.
  • A PDF that carries JavaScript, an embedded executable or a launch action is refused rather than opened.

You see and can change every line before anything is saved.

Notifications

Reminders you create are scheduled by your device and delivered by your device. No reminder is sent through a server, and the app has no push-notification token and no ability to send you anything remotely. Notification permission is optional; if you refuse it, reminders are still saved, and the app tells you they will not alert.

Backups and exports

You can export your data at any time, as a CSV file, a readable summary, or a full backup. An export goes wherever you send it through your phone's own share sheet. Once it leaves the app it is an ordinary file in your control, and this policy no longer governs it — if you put it in a cloud drive, that provider's terms apply.

Full backups can be encrypted with a passphrase you choose. Encryption uses scrypt for key derivation and XChaCha20-Poly1305 for the data. The passphrase is never stored and cannot be recovered: if you lose it, that backup cannot be opened, by us or by anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything removes every entry, goal, reminder, debt, import record and learned word from the device, and returns the app to the state it was in when you installed it. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the device. (An iPhone backup you made earlier keeps what was there when it was made, until you delete that backup.)

Because OnlyBudget keeps no copy of your data off your device, there is nothing to request deletion of from us, and no request to make.

Children

OnlyBudget is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. It collects no personal information from any user, of any age.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, portability, deletion, and the right to know whether it is sold.

The developer of OnlyBudget holds no personal data about you. There is no user record, no profile and no identifier. Your data is in your possession, on your device, and only you can read it. In practical terms:

  • Access and portability: everything is in the app, and the export produces a complete, readable copy in a standard format.
  • Correction: every entry is editable.
  • Deletion: Settings → Erase everything, or uninstall.
  • Sale or sharing: your data is never sold, shared, rented or disclosed. No mechanism exists by which it could be.
